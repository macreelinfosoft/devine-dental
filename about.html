About Our Clinic

  • Dr. Akansha is a highly skilled and compassionate general dentist with a special focus on cosmetic dentistry. With her extensive training and experience, she is dedicated to providing quality dental care to her patients in a gentle and welcoming manner.
  • One of Dr. Akansha's areas of expertise is cosmetic dentistry. She offers a range of services to enhance the appearance of her patients' smiles, including Invisalign, which helps straighten teeth discreetly; veneers, which can improve the shape and color of teeth; and teeth whitening, to create a brighter, more youthful smile. Dr. Akansha understands the importance of a confident smile and strives to help her patients achieve the aesthetic results they desire.
  • In addition to cosmetic procedures, Dr. Akansha also focuses on restorative dentistry. She provides solutions for missing teeth, such as dentures, implants, crowns, and bridges. These treatments not only restore the function of the teeth but also improve the overall appearance of the smile. Dr. Akansha believes in the importance of preventing major dental issues and offers composite/ceramic fillings, sealants, root canal treatments, and scalings to maintain optimal oral health.
  • Dr. Akansha obtained a Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 2014 to embark on a lifelong journey toward becoming a skilled dentist
  • Dr. Akansha's dedication to her profession goes beyond her clinical practice. She is committed to staying up-to-date with the latest advancements in dentistry and regularly attends conferences and workshops across Australia. By continuously expanding her dental knowledge, she ensures that her patients receive the most innovative and effective treatments available.
  • Creating a warm and friendly dental atmosphere is a top priority for Dr. Akansha. She understands that many people may feel anxious or fearful about visiting the dentist, so she strives to create a comfortable environment where patients can feel at ease. Dr. Akansha's gentle approach and friendly demeanor help build trust and rapport with her patients, allowing them to relax during their dental appointments.
  • Outside of her dental practice, Dr. Akansha enjoys listening to music and cherishes spending quality time with her family. Her passion for dentistry and commitment to her patients is evident in her dedication to ongoing education and her efforts to create a positive dental experience for everyone she treats.
